# Ep01 — "Third Wheel in Space" — Outline

**Source:** Mr. & Mrs. X #2 (Marvel, 2018), "Love & Marriage" part 2 of 5. Kelly Thompson (writer),
Oscar Bazaldua (artist), Frank D'Armata (colors), Travis Lanham (letters). Cover by Terry & Rachel Dodson.
Phillip's own copy: 22 pages, panels/p001–p022.

**Target length:** 9–10 min (≈1,300 narration words + about 10 short character lines)

**Angle / commentary hook:** A honeymoon episode that's really about trust. Rogue and Gambit just got
married, and the issue throws the worst possible third wheel at them: Deadpool, Rogue's old flirt from
her Avengers days. Our take: this is Gambit's jealousy getting tested in real time, and Rogue running
the field while both men talk too much. And it all ends on a cliffhanger that changes the whole series.

**Cast (voices):** NARRATOR, GAMBIT (both designed), ROGUE and DEADPOOL (need designing).
Deathbird, Gladiator and Technet are retold by the narrator, with no extra voices.

## Beats
1. **Cold open: the crash** (~0:45) — p004, p006–p007. A ship gets shot down and falls toward a red planet.
   Gambit, handcuffed on an enemy bridge, is counting on Rogue's invulnerability. Then BOOM, and Deadpool
   coughs his way out of the wreck. Hook: *"Why is Deadpool on Rogue's honeymoon?"*
2. **Previously on Mr. & Mrs. X** (~1:00) — p001–p002. Quick recap in our words: the surprise wedding
   after Kitty and Piotr's fell through, a honeymoon in space, a mysterious package, the Shi'ar Imperial
   Guard, and Rogue blacking out and waking up next to Deadpool.
3. **On the Guard's bridge** (~1:15) — p003–p006. The Guard has taken the couple's honeymoon ship.
   Gladiator orders the package destroyed rather than lose it. Gambit picks his cuffs. Deathbird turns
   out to have a hidden teleporter and takes the whole Guard out with her. Gambit is left alone on the
   bridge and heads down after Rogue.
4. **Rogue and Wade** (~1:30) — p007–p009. Deadpool's teleport beam saved Rogue in space, so now he has
   a rescue to brag about. He's after the "egg," which is worth a hundred million dollars, and he's
   hurt he wasn't invited to the wedding. Commentary: why Rogue and Deadpool's history works as comedy,
   and why it lands on Gambit. Gambit arrives, and Deadpool swipes the egg.
5. **"Do I have your permission to blow him up?"** (~1:15) — p010–p013. Gambit finally gets to blow
   Deadpool up, with Rogue's blessing. The "crystal" was only a case, and inside is an egg. Wade wants
   the money, Rogue punches him, and he answers with a grenade. Gambit loses patience fast.
6. **Technet crashes the party** (~1:45) — p014–p019. A bounty-hunter team with ridiculous powers
   (quick tour: the shrinker, the telepathic "mother" Gatecrasher and her teleporter Yap, the mucus guy,
   the nightmare baby). The three of them call a truce. Gambit takes down Gatecrasher, Deadpool's mouth
   scares off the nightmare baby, and Rogue absorbs Yap's teleportation and sends Technet packing.
   The parting warning: everything in the Shi'ar galaxy is coming for this egg.
7. **Cabin fever** (~1:00) — p020–p021. Back on the ship, Deadpool wants to be dropped off with "his"
   egg, Gambit says absolutely not, and Rogue tells Wade to walk away. Then: the egg is cracking.
8. **The reveal + our take** (~1:15) — p021–p022. The egg hatches into a young woman who looks a lot
   like Rogue and asks for help. Our wrap-up: what Thompson does with a third wheel, how the issue treats
   trust in a new marriage, and the big question: who is she?
9. **Outro** (~0:20) — tease the next episode, ask viewers who they think she is, and like/subscribe.
