NARRATOR: Here's my take on this issue. On paper it's a chase comic. A package, a bounty, a fight in the desert. But Kelly Thompson is really writing about the first week of a marriage. Gambit's jealousy is right there on the surface. Rogue's past keeps walking into the room, and she keeps choosing to handle it instead of hiding it.
